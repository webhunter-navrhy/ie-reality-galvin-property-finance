Description

Galvin Property are delighted to bring No. 22 Park Drive Grove to the open market for sale. This spacious two-bedroom mid-terrace property enjoys a great location, with off-street parking and a sizeable rear garden.

The front door opens into a spacious living room, with stairs to the first-floor landing and understairs storage. The living room leads through to the kitchen/diner, which has a door out to the rear garden. Upstairs, there are two generously sized double bedrooms and a family bathroom. Bedroom one is a sizeable double with a window to the front, while bedroom two is again a sizeable double, with a window to the rear. The family bathroom is fitted with a Velux roof light. To the front, there is ample & private space for off-street parking.

The location is excellent, with Castleknock Village and all its amenities just a short stroll away. A good selection of primary schools is within walking distance, including the ever-popular St Brigid's National School (school admission policies are subject to change and should be verified independently). Transport links are excellent too, with Castleknock Train Station just a five-minute walk away and a number of Dublin Bus routes on hand. The M50 is a two-minute drive, making trips into the city and further afield easy.

While the property requires full refurbishment, it offers a new owner a blank canvas to create a stunning home in a highly popular area.
